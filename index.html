What I work on

Research Interests

Production Scheduling

Flexible job shop, flow shop and dual-resource (machine/operator) scheduling problems from real manufacturing settings.

Combinatorial Optimization

Mixed-integer programming and constraint programming models for large-scale discrete decision problems.

Exact & Decomposition Methods

Benders and Logic-Based Benders Decomposition, and hybrid MIP/CP schemes that make hard problems tractable.

Metaheuristics

Local search, tabu search and variable neighborhood search with graph-based neighborhood structures for high-quality solutions within tight time limits.

Optimization under Uncertainty

Stochastic programming with the L-shaped method and advanced stochastic decompositions, plus stochastic heuristics for operational uncertainty in real-world planning.

Human-Centric & Sustainable Manufacturing

Scheduling with human-resource side constraints, workforce-aware planning (Industry 5.0) and sustainability measures in production systems.

Where I've worked

Experience

  1. Feb 2022 – Present

    PhD Researcher & Algorithm Developer

    Ghent University & Flanders Make · Ghent, Belgium

    • Architected and implemented high-performance C++ scheduling engines for human-centric Flexible Job Shop Scheduling (FJSP).
    • Formulated deterministic and stochastic optimization models addressing operational uncertainty, workforce side constraints and dual-resource machine/operator allocation.
    • Implemented exact decomposition schemes (Logic-Based Benders Decomposition, MIP, CP) and local search metaheuristics, improving solution quality and cutting runtime on large-scale benchmarks.
